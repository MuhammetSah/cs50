Yu-Gi-Oh! is a highly competitive, fast-paced Japanese trading card game (TCG) where two players face off in a tactical duel. Originally based on a manga and anime series, the game is centered around summoning monsters, casting spells, and setting traps to defeat your opponent.

I've been playing this game for over 20 years now. Me and my friends are still active in playing and use every opportunity to go for the National Championships and so on.
